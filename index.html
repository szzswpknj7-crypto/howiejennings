THE STORY

SOME SONGS
HAVE TO LIVE.

Nashville songwriter Howie Jennings writes songs for other artists and the hard truth is, most of those songs will never see the light of day. But “Beer League” was one he couldn’t let stay on the shelf.

Written with teammate and fellow songwriter Matt Roy and produced by hitmaker and Beer League goalie Chris DeStefano, it’s a love letter to the game and to everyone you’re lucky enough to keep lacing them up with.

THE STORY

SOME SONGS
HAVE TO LIVE.

Nashville songwriter Howie Jennings writes songs for other artists and the hard truth is, most of those songs will ever see the light of day. But “Beer League” was one he couldn’t let stay on the shelf.

Written with teammate, and prolific songwriter, Matt Roy and produced by hitmaker and Howie's Beer League goalie, Chris DeStefano, it’s a love letter to the game, and everyone you get the honour of continuing to lace them up with.
